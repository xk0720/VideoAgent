## 4. Music-to-video FIT practice

- **Music bed level**: professional consensus is music **-18 to -20 dB below dialogue/narration** (usable range -15 to -25; <15 dB gap masks speech, >25 dB inaudible) ([pureaudioinsight.com](https://pureaudioinsight.com/blogs/content-production/background-music-volume-how-loud-should-it-be), [descript.com](https://www.descript.com/blog/article/how-to-set-the-perfect-audio-levels-for-video)). NLEs (Premiere Essential Sound) automate this as "ducking against dialogue" ([premieregal.com](https://premieregal.com/blog/2021/11/12/how-to-mix-sound-for-video-in-premiere-pro)).
- **Platform loudness**: YouTube normalizes to ≈ **-14 LUFS integrated** (turns down louder, never up) ([criticallisteninglab.com/learn/loudness/youtube](https://www.criticallisteninglab.com/en/learn/loudness/youtube)); 2026 guidance: master shorts/TikTok/Reels to **-14 LUFS, -1.0 dBTP ceiling** as platforms now aggressively turn down hot masters ([mrvocal.com](https://mrvocal.com/posts/loudness-for-shorts), [clickyapps.com](https://clickyapps.com/creator/video/guides/lufs-targets-2025)); some older sources say TikTok tolerates -12 to -10.
- **Beat alignment**: industry practice aligns cuts to beats but "not every beat needs a cut — narrative flow first" ([beat2cut.com](https://beat2cut.com/blog/beat-sync-video-editing-complete-guide/)); a Facebook-attributed figure claims up to 40% higher completion with strong AV sync (secondary source, [beat2cut.com](https://beat2cut.com/blog/beat-sync-video-editing-complete-guide/)) — no rigorous peer-reviewed effect size found for short narrative film specifically. Active research exists: BEAT, rhythm-elastic music-guided trailer editing ([arxiv.org/pdf/2605.27067](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.27067)). Practical implication for Maestro: since cuts are fixed by the shot plan, fitting music TO the cuts (video-to-music) is the right direction, not cutting to music.

## 5. Fully-local zero-cost chain — feasible, all recipes established

- **Beat/tempo analysis**: `librosa.beat.beat_track` — Ellis 2007 dynamic-programming tracker (onset strength → tempo autocorrelation → DP peak-picking), handles time-varying tempo ([librosa.org beat_track docs](https://librosa.org/doc/0.11.0/generated/librosa.beat.beat_track.html), [Ellis paper](https://www.ee.columbia.edu/~dpwe/pubs/Ellis07-beattrack.pdf)). ~60% raw beat accuracy on eval data — fine for BPM matching, not frame-exact sync.
- **Ducking**: ffmpeg `sidechaincompress` (split voice with `asplit`, sidechain music, `amix`), e.g. `threshold=0.02:ratio=10` recipes documented ([ffmpeglab.com](https://www.ffmpeglab.com/articles/ffmpeg-audio-mixing-amix-guide.html), [ffmpeg-user thread](https://ffmpeg-user.ffmpeg.narkive.com/2wAgTY9h/audio-ducking-of-input-music-based-on-vocals-in-another-audio-file)).
- **Loudness**: **two-pass** `loudnorm` (pass 1 `print_format=json` measure → pass 2 apply measured_* with `linear=true`) to hit -14 LUFS without single-pass pumping artifacts ([dev.to two-pass loudnorm](https://dev.to/masonwritescode/two-pass-loudness-normalization-with-ffmpeg-loudnorm-the-right-way-1nm3)).

## Honest fit assessment for Maestro (low-cost)

1. **Best fit: WaveSpeed `sonilo/video-to-music`** — same provider/API key, ~$1.50–3.24 per finished video, music auto-fitted to pacing and scene transitions, eliminating beat-matching logic entirely. Run once on the final concat, then local ffmpeg sidechaincompress + two-pass loudnorm (-14 LUFS) for the mix. Total added cost per video: single-digit dollars max, mostly <$1 for shorts.
2. **Cheapest API: ACE-Step 1.5 on WaveSpeed** (<$0.02/min, MIT, explicit BPM tags) or free local if a GPU box exists — but it's text-prompted, so the brain must describe mood/BPM per act and you accept looser fit.
3. **Zero-cost fallback: Pixabay tracks** (manual/scripted curation, commercial-safe, no attribution) + librosa BPM check + the same ffmpeg mix chain.
4. **Avoid**: Suno (no official API, unofficial ones violate ToS), MusicGen (CC-BY-NC weights bar commercial use), YouTube Audio Library for multi-platform distribution (license scoped to YouTube, no API).
5. ElevenLabs ($0.15–0.50/min) and Stable Audio 2.5 ($0.20/gen) are quality upgrades if Sonilo output disappoints; both commercially cleared and both already reachable through WaveSpeed/fal without new accounts (ElevenLabs is on WaveSpeed; Stable Audio is not — it would need fal/Replicate).
